DISTAL RADIUS FRACTURES
Colles fracture is the most common fracture
of the distal radius in elderly osteoporotic women
• Dorsal angulation and dorsal displacement of the distal fragment
• Dinner fork deformity
Smith fracture is also known as reverse Colles fracture
Barton fracture is an intra-articular fracture-dislocation of the radiocarpal joint
Chauffeur fracture is a fracture of the radial styloid
Acceptable reduction: radial inclination more than 15 degrees, radial height
loss less than 5 mm, and dorsal tilt less than 10 degrees
Extensor pollicis longus rupture is a known late complication of undisplaced distal radius fractures
Median nerve is the most commonly injured nerve in distal radius fractures
Complex regional pain syndrome is associated with tight casts and is treated early with vitamin C prophylaxis
